# Story and boss progression decisions

Confirmed by the user on November 1, 2026. Read alongside `docs/story/MegaEarth2049_Story.md`, quest implementation documents and live map events. This records specific current direction; it does not replace the existing story with a new plot.

## Follow the existing story

Use the established story, characters, locations, Necklace quest, A.S.P./Protocol Zero conflict and ending choices as the foundation. Small changes and additions are welcome when they fit new encounters into that story. Before inventing a new motive, gate, location or plot branch, look for the existing equivalent. Explain and document substantive deviations rather than silently rewriting progression.

Story documents include proposed content that is not yet in the game and occasional contradictions. Distinguish authored intent from live implementation. Resolve conflicts using the user's explicit direction, then reconcile the relevant text and mechanics; do not treat a convenient new implementation as authority to discard the story. Older alternate endgame and optional/postgame outlines are not permission to relocate or replace the confirmed finale.

## Boss hierarchy and finale

- There will be several regional main bosses across world-map-type areas. They use the main-boss workflow and may have multiple combat phases; being a main boss does not make one the game's final boss.
- The Underwriter is a regional boss for the Dorlund branch of Planet Ork (world Map028), not either final boss. It concludes the existing missing-miners/payroll/denied-claims branch. It does not replace the separately authored fire-breathing scorpion Necklace guardian in the Act 3 story.
- At the end of the game, the party returns to the first world map: Map001, Mega Earth.
- The party must gain entry to and navigate through the floating castle before the final encounter sequence. Existing relevant mapped locations are named Floating Mansion: Map038 exterior, Map040 first floor, Map091 second floor and associated rooms. Reconcile the castle terminology with these established locations and the story's tower/core approach when building the finale; do not invent another planet or discard existing entry quests.
- The very end has **two bosses back to back**, followed by the story's ending resolution. These are two encounters, not merely two phases of one enemy. Their final identities, exact map placement, recovery/checkpoint policy and handoff implementation are not yet established. Prefer existing A.S.P.-related story roles when designing them.
- Preserve the Necklace's role and existing entry/resource requirements, including the authored Golden Gear/Completed Engine route where applicable. Regional victories and six service mandates must not automatically complete the Necklace, bypass castle traversal, award an ending, or substitute for the final pair.
- The existing A.S.P. physical/digital defenses, creator revelations, Memory Labyrinth and ending choices remain story material to integrate into that return-and-castle sequence. The confirmed route constrains their eventual placement; it is not an instruction to delete them.

## Installed regional integration

The Underwriter is reached from the Payroll Ossuary (Map115) after the erased crew roster is recovered (switch125, or completed quest switch127). Either public-memorial or private-family-copy choice qualifies. Map238 is the local Executive Liability chamber; victory switch174 opens Map239, the Claims Audit Archive. Mags, the twelve missing workers, the false voluntary-absence records and the families' claims come from the existing Dorlund quest. The Underwriter's role is the small addition that personifies the existing corporate obstruction.

This route is local to Planet Ork. It has no corridor to the MegaEarth service vestibule. The six custodian mandates and Maps236–237 retain their existing service-access function, but they do not implement the floating-castle finale. Future changes must preserve that distinction.
