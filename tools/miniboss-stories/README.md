# Six custodians of the service route

Status: narrative setup installed in twelve existing room-marker events. No battles, new locks, victory flags, rewards, or endgame maps are installed by this pass. Combat/art and the gates below are authored specifications for the next implementation pass. Existing quests, disclosure choices, rewards, exits, and Repossessor remain intact.

## Connection to the final goal

The five MegaCorps outsourced essential services to six custodians. NeuraTech reused their legacy authority system to supply its Core. Rex can reclaim that authority and reach a service vestibule on the approach to A.S.P. and Protocol Zero. This is an additional route, not a replacement for the Necklace, Golden Gear/Beanstalk progression, Dr. Voss research, or the four endings in MegaEarth2049_GameStory.md. The final Core is still future content.

The six local confrontations can be found in any order once their existing evidence quests permit access. Proposed local rule: recover the evidence, confront the custodian, win, then open its deeper service hatch and grant its mandate. Public disclosure and private copies qualify equally. Previously completed saves qualify from their existing recovered-evidence flag. No player must repeat or reverse an ethical choice.

Identity + Repair open the inland junction approach; Routing + Flow open the waterfront approach. These are alternative approaches to one junction, not cross-planet walking tunnels. Interplanetary travel remains on existing routes; mandates are credentials carried by the party. Wake and Thermal authorize the neural and cooling relays there; all six are required for the optional Core service entrance. Each local victory also opens its own deeper inspection path, so every boss provides immediate access as well as overall progress. New room maps and transfers must be built in their actual local geography.

Implementation contract: allocate six unused victory switches against live references when combat is built; never set them from reading a file or accepting a quest. On victory set one switch, open the gate, and give any reward once. Loss/retreat leaves the gate locked and the original exit available. The junction reports missing mandates by name. No boss is placed on an existing escape tile. Keep the current six evidence quests completable before activating new encounters.

Theme count: all six use corporate disillusionment. The first four emphasize STD and disease satire (real STD names plus clearly fictional biotech conditions), the fifth uses neurological body horror; the sixth uses cold corporate cruelty and mechanical horror. The target of illness jokes is exploitation and manufactured suffering, not ordinary patients.

## Chief Compliance Orifice — Please State Your Name Into the Wound

NeuraTech · Pronoun Reclamation Vault · teaser Map122, confrontation approach Map127, marker event 1. Future arena branches from this room; do not replace its recovery console.

**Body:** A desk-bound registrar grown into a wet accordion of stolen faces. Its designer infection, Terms and Conditions, prints licensing clauses across weeping sores. NeuraTech bundled this fictional STD with its intimacy implants: even the rash has a copyright notice. Rubber-stamp teeth chew applications while a throat full of name badges announces the wrong employee.

**Hook:** Clerk Alias discovers that returning the originals has not deleted the corporate ownership copies. The registrar is still leasing residents their own names by the syllable.

**Clue:** NEURATECH: Our intimacy implant gave you Terms and Conditions. That is the rash, not the contract. Scratching constitutes acceptance.

**Dossier:** The Chief Compliance Orifice keeps the Identity mandate behind Originals Access. Its intimacy-implant STD prints contracts on skin. NeuraTech owns the rash and charges to redact it. Recovering personal records does not cancel its ownership copies or its Core service authority.

**Intro:** COMPLIANCE: State your full name, employee number, and preferred orifice for receiving corrections.

**Reply:** REX: Permission denied. That is my face on your ass.

**Defeat:** COMPLIANCE: Your complaint has been swallowed. Estimated digestion: six to eight working lifetimes.

**Aftermath:** The stamp stops. Names remain names; nobody needs corporate approval to use them. A surviving clerk can revoke the ownership copies.

**Gate:** Victory opens the Identity service hatch behind Originals Access. Its audit corridor points to the Warranty Intestine and Misdelivery Repository. Other existing dungeon doors stay open.

**Mechanics:** Proposed: a named anticipation turn before Silence; ordinary physical damage between stamps. No name, gender, actor identity, or player-input changes.

**Art:** Organic: throat contractions, faces shifting under the skin, stamp-jaw snap. The desk and badge rail remain rigid.

Companion reply is conditional on that actor being in the party; use Rex narration otherwise. Intro/defeat/aftermath are authored for the future encounter, not currently played by the dossier.

## The Deductible Duke — Pre-existing You

Vitalix / Armatek · Warranty Intestine · teaser Map140, confrontation approach Map145, marker event 1. Future arena branches from this room; do not replace its recovery console.

**Body:** A powdered executive wig perched on a walking colony of swollen glands and glossy corporate sores. Spare elbows hold its split insurance-policy skin together. A chest-mounted claims printer spits denial receipts through an ulcer. It sells premium herpes management, gonorrhea add-ons, and a syphilis rider that excludes having syphilis. The monstrous anatomy is engineered biotech fantasy.

**Hook:** Mechanic Socket finds that the recall fix was only temporary: a Vitalix claims engine can remotely lock repaired joints again. Armatek calls this recurring revenue.

**Clue:** VITALIX: Having a body is a pre-existing condition. Your claim is denied. Please stop bleeding on the exclusions.

**Dossier:** The Deductible Duke hoards the Repair mandate in Recall Access. Having a body is officially a pre-existing condition. Its STD coverage excludes infection, symptoms, and treatment. Repossessor collects hardware; the Duke writes the policy and controls the remote joint locks.

**Intro:** DUKE: Having a body is a pre-existing condition. Herpes? Excluded. Gonorrhea? Excluded. The consultation explaining this? Billable.

**Reply:** JEN: I have diagnosed you with terminal middle management.

**Defeat:** DUKE: This discharge is not medically necessary. Nor, apparently, voluntary.

**Aftermath:** Socket disconnects the recall veto. Residents keep their repairs without paying to rent functioning knees.

**Gate:** Victory opens the Recall maintenance lift and grants Repair authority. Together with Identity, it enables the dry inland approach to the final maintenance junction.

**Mechanics:** Proposed: telegraphed HP drain and a temporary DEF buff that expires normally. No equipment destruction, permanent injuries, insurance currency, or real medical advice.

**Art:** Organic glands swell and ulcer-mouths argue over coverage; fixed metal joint housings articulate without changing size. Receipts feed from the chest printer rather than stretch. Distinct silhouette from Repossessor.
