Quest 1: The Mysterious Necklace
Category: 1
Difficulty: 2
From: Found in Timbuc Cellar
Location: Various locations throughout the game
Description: You've discovered a strange necklace piece in the Timbuc Cellar. It seems to be part of a larger artifact with mysterious powers. Collect all the pieces to unlock its true potential.
Objectives:
- Find the necklace piece in Timbuc Cellar
- Locate the second piece in the Abandoned Mine
- Discover the third piece in the Floating Mansion
- Find the final piece in the A.S.P. Facility
- Assemble the complete necklace
Rewards:
- Complete Necklace (Key Item)
- Enhanced neural implant capabilities
- Access to new areas
Subtext: The necklace appears to interact with your neural implant in strange ways.
Quotes:
- "This artifact predates the corporate wars. Handle with care." - Zed
- "I've seen symbols like these before, in the old Earth archives." - Jen

Quest 2: The Engine Reconstruction
Category: 2
Difficulty: 3
From: Armatek Engineer in Timbuc Cellar
Location: Various locations
Description: An Armatek engineer needs your help to reconstruct an advanced engine. The components are scattered across different locations, and you'll need to find them all to complete the engine.
Objectives:
- Obtain the engine blueprint from the Armatek engineer
- Find Component A at the construction site
- Find Component B from a malfunctioning A.S.P. drone
- Find Component C from the Armatek office
- Return all components to the engineer
Rewards:
- Advanced Engine (Key Item)
- Engineering skill increase
- 500 credits
Subtext: This engine design seems unusually advanced for current technology. The engineer believes it could revolutionize transportation in the wasteland.
Quotes:
- "With this engine, we could revolutionize transportation in the wasteland." - Armatek Engineer
- "I haven't seen technology like this since before the collapse." - Zed

Quest 9: The Mysterious Smelly Keys
Category: 3
Difficulty: 2
From: Found in Timbuc Cellar
Location: Timbuc Cellar
Description: Strange, smelly keys have been scattered throughout Timbuc Cellar. Each key unlocks a specific door, potentially leading to valuable treasures.
Objectives:
- Find Smelly Key #1 in central Timbuc Cellar
- Unlock the door to Map020 using Smelly Key #1
- Find Smelly Key #2 and unlock the door to Map021
- Find Smelly Key #3 and unlock the door to Map017
- Find Smelly Key #4 and unlock the door to Map032
- Find Smelly Key #5 and unlock the door to Map018
- Find Smelly Key #6 and unlock the door to Map019
- Find Smelly Key #7 and unlock the door to Map016
Rewards:
- Access to hidden areas with valuable items
- 1000 credits total (distributed across the hidden areas)
- Unique equipment items in the final area
Subtext: These keys have a distinct, unpleasant smell. Who would create such foul-smelling keys, and what treasures might they protect?
Quotes:
- "I've heard rumors of hidden treasures in Timbuc Cellar, but the smell of those keys is almost not worth it." - Timbuc Resident
- "Each key has a unique smell. Some say the smell corresponds to what's behind each door." - Mysterious Vendor

Quest 11: The Ancient Necklace
Category: 4
Difficulty: 2
From: Found in Timbuc Cellar
Location: Timbuc Cellar
Description: Ancient necklace pieces have been scattered throughout Timbuc Cellar. According to local legends, when combined, these pieces form a powerful artifact that grants enhanced abilities to the wearer.
Objectives:
- Find Necklace Piece 1 in Timbuc Cellar
- Find Necklace Piece 2 in Timbuc Cellar
- Find Necklace Piece 3 in Timbuc Cellar

Quest 4: Zed's Clinic
Category: 4
Difficulty: 2
From: Zed
Location: Zed's Clinic in Timbuc
Description: Zed, the eccentric cybernetic surgeon, needs rare materials for his experimental treatments. Help him gather these materials and you might benefit from his unique skills.
Objectives:

- Obtain synthetic tissue from the Corporate District

- Access to Zed's special inventory
Subtext: Zed's methods are unorthodox, but his results are undeniable. Just don't ask where he learned his techniques.
Quotes:

- "I've never seen someone's body accept the implants so well!" - Zed
